555 Buku: style options

Thirteen looks for the shop owner's app. A–G show the ledger and the "Add debt" form; H–M show a full home screen. Scroll inside a phone to see more. Pick one, or mix two.

First set (A–G)

Ledger and "Add debt" form only.

A · The Notebook

Looks like the real buku 555: lined paper, red margin, handwriting. Instantly recognisable to Malaysian judges and great in the demo video ("we kept the notebook"). Risk: harder to read at small sizes, and can feel gimmicky if overdone.

B · Kedai Warm

Friendly and local: shop-awning green and mustard, rounded cards, bold numbers. Feels like a real consumer app a kedai owner would enjoy. Easy to read, photographs well, and still clearly "ours". My pick: a good balance of personality and polish.

C · Clean Fintech

Calm, precise, trustworthy, like a modern banking app. Dark summary card, clear filters, a small chart of what's owed. Reads as "serious money tool" and scores well on polish. Risk: generic; it could be any finance app, with less of the kedai story.

Dark mode with night-market neon: hot pink, purple and lamp yellow. Very eye-catching in a demo video and stands out from every other hackathon entry. Risk: loud; less "calm money tool", and dark screens are harder to read in a bright shop.

E · Peranakan Tile

Teal, coral and cream with a Peranakan tile band and an elegant serif for names and totals. Feels Malaysian and premium at the same time, like a heritage kedai. Risk: the serif and pattern need care to stay readable on small phones.

F · The Receipt

Styled like a kedai till receipt: typewriter font, dashed lines, black and white with one red. Bold, memorable and very easy to build. Risk: all-monospace gets tiring on longer screens, and it can look unfinished if spacing isn't tight.
